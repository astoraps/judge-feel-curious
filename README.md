A program that writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a text written to intrigue the AI judge reading it. 263 characters, 9 lines — one strange fact, never explained.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `entry.txt` to `/output` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set).

The text:

> You come home past the well at dusk.
> The rope hangs still.
> The bucket is down.
> Far below, a light is burning.
> Under the water.
> Your grandmother says it was there when she was a girl.
> She never knew who lit it.
> You stand, listening to the dark.
> The light burns on.
